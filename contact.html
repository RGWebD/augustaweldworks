FAQ

Good questions.

Do you offer mobile welding?

Yes. Onsite services are listed on the Augusta Weld Works Google profile, and customers mention mobile service in their reviews, including a next-day trailer repair done on site.

Where is Augusta Weld Works?

The listed address is 204 Plymouth Rd, Augusta, GA 30907. Call (706) 550-5298 to set up onsite service or to bring a trailer or piece of equipment by.

What are your hours?

Open every day, 6 AM to 6 PM Eastern. Holiday hours may vary: Thanksgiving and the day after are flagged on Google as “hours might differ,” so call ahead around holidays.

Can I get an estimate?

Online estimates are listed on the Google profile. Call (706) 550-5298 and describe the job, or use the estimate form on the contact page to send the details.

What kind of welding do you do?

Trailer repair (landing gear, gates, hinges, tailgates, rails, floors and spring hangers), ramps and ladder racks, ornamental railings and gates, mailbox posts, mower, bike and go-kart frames, and custom fabrication.

How fast can you get to my job?

It depends on the schedule. Customers describe same-day repairs and a next-day onsite visit, and one says the shop “will always attempt to offer same-day service.” Call to check today’s availability.

How much does a weld repair cost?

Prices aren’t listed online. Customers describe the pricing as fair and reasonable. Call (706) 550-5298 with the details for a quote.
